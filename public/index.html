Mzansi Movers

Free WhatsApp-based exercise therapy for South Africans living with Parkinson's Disease

What We Do

Mzansi Movers delivers guided exercise sessions via WhatsApp, making physiotherapy accessible to anyone with a phone. Our programme is designed specifically for people with Parkinson's Disease and is completely free.

How It Works

  • Message our WhatsApp bot to get started
  • Complete a short screening questionnaire
  • Receive personalised exercise videos based on your mobility level
  • Track your progress over time

Why WhatsApp?

WhatsApp is the most widely used messaging app in South Africa. By meeting people where they already are, we remove barriers to accessing exercise therapy — no app downloads, no data-heavy websites, no cost.

Privacy Policy

Mzansi Movers takes your privacy seriously and complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

  • No personal data stored: We never store your phone number. All user data is anonymised using one-way hashing.
  • Minimal data collection: We only collect information needed to personalise your exercise programme (age range, mobility level, exercise history).
  • No sharing: Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Your rights: You can request deletion of your data at any time by messaging "DELETE" to the bot.
  • Security: All data is stored in secured, encrypted cloud infrastructure.
